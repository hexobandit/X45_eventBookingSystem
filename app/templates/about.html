We started {{ site_name }} after years of answering the same questions from colleagues: how do you light this, why does that flash look flat, what would you do with one softbox and a white wall. The answers were always easier to show than to write.

So we built a studio where we can show them. Every course here is taught on real equipment, with real subjects, in groups small enough that nobody waits for a turn.
